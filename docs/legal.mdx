## Measurements and comparisons

Every number on this site was measured as described on [Benchmarks](/benchmarks) and [Lessons](/lessons), mostly on one machine and in one or two runs; your repositories and machines will differ. [Compared](/compare) reads each tool from its own documentation on the dates given there. Those tools change, so a cell can go out of date after the date it was read.
